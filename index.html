Product PagePet nutritionMetric on fire: Add-to-Cart

Leading the PDP with "how to use it" lifted add-to-cart 7.54%

The signal

The Feeding Guide accordion earned a strong click rate despite sitting dead last, below Description, Nutrition, and Ingredients. Shoppers were hunting for practical "how do I use this" info and having to dig for it.

Hypothesis

If practical usage context appears right after "what it is," hesitation drops and more shoppers add to cart. So I moved the Feeding Guide to second, directly under Description.
