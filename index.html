Landing PagePolar Hair CareMetric on fire: Listicle to PDP drop-off

Putting the buy box on the listicle lifted revenue per visitor 12.8%

The signal

The top paid-social listicle did the selling and then sent everyone to the product page to buy. Only about a quarter of listicle sessions got there, and the product page converted around 8% of those. Most of the intent the page built was lost on the way to the cart.

Hypothesis

If people can buy where they are, a page load and a decision drop out of the funnel. I rebuilt the product page's buy box as theme snippets (gallery, price, shade picker, bottle cards, the rewards ladder, add to cart) and placed it after the customer results, with every CTA on the page scrolling to it.
